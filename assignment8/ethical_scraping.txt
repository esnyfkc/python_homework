**Restricted Sections:**

Wikipedia restricts access to areas such as `/w/`, `/api/`, `/trap/`, and `/wiki/Special:`. It also restricts certain deletion, copyright, administration, discussion, and noticeboard pages.

**Specific User-Agent Rules:**

Some user agents are completely blocked from crawling Wikipedia. These include MJ12bot, HTTrack, and wget. SemrushBot is permitted to crawl the site, but it must follow a crawl delay of 5 seconds.

**Reflection:**

The `robots.txt` file informs web crawlers which parts of a website they may or may not access. Following these rules helps reduce unnecessary server traffic and prevents crawlers from overloading the website or accessing restricted areas.
